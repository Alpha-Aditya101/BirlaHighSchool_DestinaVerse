Plan with confidence

One day, paced around the city.

Arrive before 08:00 for the quieter Sun Gate ceremony, take lunch in the Craft Quarter between 13:00 and 14:00, and reach the riverfront before 18:30 to catch the lanterns coming on. Wear comfortable shoes: the full route covers about 7.3 kilometres.
