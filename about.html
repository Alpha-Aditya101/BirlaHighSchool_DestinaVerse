One city,

Our purpose

Keep the past close while making the future easier to explore.

Suryapur's Digital Museum & Smart City Portal brings together the information people need to understand, visit and participate in their city. The project is designed for citizens, visitors, students and researchers.

Every page is made to be clear, useful and open to the people whose stories belong here.
